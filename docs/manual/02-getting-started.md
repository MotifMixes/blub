# Getting started

1. Start Blub (`Blub.exe` on Windows, or `blub-gui` when you built it yourself).
2. Click **Choose games folder** and pick any cartridge in the folder where your dumps are. Blub looks there and in its subfolders, and makes a picture of each game's title screen.
3. Click a game to play it.

You can also open a single cartridge with **File › Open Cartridge…** (`Ctrl+O`), or drop it on the window.

## The keys

The piano's 36 keys are on three rows of the PC keyboard; Enter, Home and the arrows are its main buttons. See [Playing](#playing) for all of them, or **Help › Controls** (`F1`) in Blub.

> Tip: a MIDI keyboard plugged in before or during a game plays the piano's keys: see [MIDI keyboards](#midi).

## Back to the library

**File › Close Cartridge** goes back to the games. `Esc` pauses.
