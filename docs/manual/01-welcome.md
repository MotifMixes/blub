# Welcome to Blub

Blub is an emulator for Fisher-Price consoles. It plays the **Fisher-Price I Can Play Piano** (2006), the TV keyboard with 36 light-up keys and plug-in song cartridges. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

This guide is built into Blub (**Help › User Guide**). Choose a page on the left, or type in the search box to find every section that mentions a word.

## What you need

Blub comes with **no games**. The I Can Play Piano has no system software of its own: each cartridge holds the whole program, so dumps of your own **cartridges** (8 MB `.bin` files) are all you need. Nothing else has to be installed.

## Made with AI

Blub is written almost entirely by an AI coding assistant (Anthropic's Claude), under the direction of its author. Blub shows a short note about this the first time it starts.

Blub is not affiliated with or endorsed by Fisher-Price or Mattel.
