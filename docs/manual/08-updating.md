# Updating Blub

When a new version is out, a blue button appears right after the menus: **⬆ Update to v…**. Click it to see what's new, then **Update now**: Blub downloads the new version, installs it and restarts. Your settings and save states are kept (and copied aside first).

**Help › Check for updates** looks at once; **Help › Patch notes** shows every release.
